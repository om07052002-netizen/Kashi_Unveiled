FAQ

Quick answers.

A few basics for first-time visitors. Always verify time-sensitive details with official sources.

How many days should I plan?

One day can cover highlights, while 2–3 days gives more room for food, heritage and Sarnath. Longer stays make it easier to explore slowly.

What is Sarnath?

Sarnath is a major heritage destination near Varanasi and is commonly included as a day or half-day visit.

Should I stay near the ghats?

It depends on your priorities. Staying closer to the old city can make riverfront exploration convenient, while other areas may offer easier road access and different hotel options.

Are all timings fixed?

No. Opening hours, ceremonies, access rules, traffic and local conditions can change. Check current official information before relying on a schedule.
