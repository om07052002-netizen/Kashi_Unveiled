Plan Your Trip

Ready-made days for different kinds of travellers.

Use these as starting points, then adjust them around your arrival time, interests and pace.

🌅

1 Day

For a first taste of Kashi.

Begin around the Ganga and ghats, explore the old-city area, make time for a major temple visit where appropriate, and finish with an evening riverfront experience.

🛕

2 Days

Heritage + riverfront.

Use day one for the ghats, old city and food. Use day two for Sarnath and additional cultural or temple experiences.

☸️

3 Days

See Kashi at a slower pace.

Add Sarnath, a second morning by the Ganga, more neighbourhood exploration and dedicated time for food and shopping.

🧭

5 Days

Go beyond the highlights.

Leave room for heritage, crafts, music, local food trails, quieter areas and flexible days for experiences you discover along the way.
